## 🔬 Research & Industrial Experience

### 🇯🇵 Nakayama Iron Works Ltd. — Japan
**International Industry Intern | MIRAI-Setu 2026 | October 2026**

Completed an eight-working-day industrial internship in Takeo, Saga, Japan.

- Explored Industrial IoT, industrial digitalisation and remote machine-monitoring systems.
- Studied the transition from traditional N-Link data processing towards the N-Global platform.
- Gained exposure to Compact Micro Hydropower Systems (CMHS), electrical configurations and technical drawings.
- Explored KEYENCE KV STUDIO and KV-8000 PLC programming, including ladder logic and basic automation.
- Analysed operational parameters of micro-hydropower installations.
- Visited Ootsubo Quarry and Matsuguma Micro Hydroelectric Power Station to observe real industrial systems.

**Focus:** IIoT | PLC | Industrial Automation | Micro Hydropower | Digitalisation

### 🔬 Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi
**Summer Research Intern | May – July 2026**

**Project: Development of a Compact Desktop Injection Moulding System for Rapid Manufacturing of Propeller Blades**

Worked on the development of a compact injection-moulding system aimed at enabling rapid manufacturing of small drone propeller blades.

- Contributed to mechanical design, CAD modelling and system development.
- Worked on mould design, aluminium tooling and mechanical assembly.
- Explored material behaviour, injection pressure, temperature regulation and mould filling.
- Investigated materials such as PDMS, liquid wax and HDPE.
- Gained exposure to CNC machining, laser cutting, 3D printing and prototyping.
- Worked in an interdisciplinary research environment involving mechanical systems and control concepts.

**Additional exposure:** Pitot-tube wind-tunnel traversing setup for velocity-profile measurements.

**Focus:** Product Development | Mechanical Design | Rapid Manufacturing | Injection Moulding | Prototyping

### 🏭 506 Army Base Workshop — Jabalpur
**Technical Intern | 2026**

Worked on a digital disassembly, training and operational-reference workflow for an artillery recoil mechanism.

- Contributed to CAD-based mechanical modelling and assembly visualisation.
- Worked on digital representation of mechanical components.
- Explored animation and technical documentation for maintenance and training applications.

**Focus:** CAD | Mechanical Assemblies | Digital Visualisation | Engineering Documentation
